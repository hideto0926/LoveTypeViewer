A living relationship map動く相関図

Select two or more people and tap “View Relationships”. Everyone is seated around the screen edge with their name facing outward — so people around the table can read it. The top-3 best and bottom-3 worst matches connect with smooth curves. Drag icons to rearrange the seats. 2人以上を選んで「関係を見る」をタップ。全員が画面の縁に着席し、名前は外側を向くので、机を囲む全員が読めます。相性の良い上位3組・悪い下位3組がなめらかな曲線でつながり、アイコンはドラッグで席替え可能。

  • Top 3 & bottom 3, drawn as smooth lines上位3・下位3をなめらかな線で表示
  • Tap a person for their 1st/2nd/3rd matches個人をタップで1・2・3位を表示
  • Two people? See the match as a %2人なら相性を％で表示
